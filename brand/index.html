Personal mastery learning · class 3–9 · Hindi & English · offline, on one device

01 · The idea

A seed that already contains the tree.

Fifteen minutes a day is a small beginning. A skill that can still be recalled a week later is the lasting growth. The whole identity is built from that one thought: the seed, its shadow and the sprout that comes out of it. Everything else stays quiet so the child's work can be loud.

Habit

One small, finishable mission a day. The streak is an invitation, never a debt.

Mastery

A skill counts as learned only when it survives review. Growth you can measure.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no network. Progress lives on the phone in the child's hand.

02 · The mark

One seed. One sprout. Four degrees of tilt.

Flat shapes only — three greens and one warm amber. No gradient, no shadow, no outline: the mark has to survive a 16 px favicon, one-ink print and embroidery without being redrawn.

03 · Lockups

Wordmark set in the reader's own sans.

The app ships no webfonts, so the wordmark is set in the system sans at weight 800 with −1 % tracking. The hyphen is always sprout green: the one coloured character, and the reason the name reads as a promise rather than a code.

07 · In the world

Sharing card and key visual.

One image does the whole introduction: what it is, who it is for, why it is safe. Both taglines travel together on anything India-facing.

Imagery rules

  • Light comes from the left; shadows fall up and to the right.
  • One seed, one shadow, one surface. Never two seeds, never a real tree in frame.
  • Warm sand and cream only: no blue skies, no classroom stock photos.
  • Type sits in the lower third over the darkened edge, left aligned.
  • The seed is never cropped, covered or used as a background texture.
  • Children appear in the product, not in the brand imagery.
